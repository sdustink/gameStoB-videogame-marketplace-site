Our Mission

We are dedicated to making game purchases simple, fast, and convenient for everyone. From the beginning, our mission is to create a convenient marketplace for gamers by ensuring excellent service, fast transactions, and easy-access to our catalog.

Our Background

gameStoB started out as a small idea founded in 2015. We were one of the first game retailer that guarenteed authentic game keys, offered new releases, DLCs, and operate a network of offline stores. Throughout our journey, the company has grown to now offering a large catalog of over 5,000 titles with multiple physical locations spread out Indonesia.
